# DEV-PLATFORM-001 AIAS Development Platform

This control plane preserves the verified AEPS V3, V4 and V5 engines behind one stable contract. It validates specification identity and workspace isolation, selects the least complex capable engine, normalizes certification and artifact evidence, creates a tamper-evident digest and publishes correlated lifecycle events compatible with CNS consumers.

Selection is explicit and auditable: V3 covers repeatable generation, V4 transactional/template production and measured certification, and V5 autonomous multidomain or incremental production. Callers may request a specific supported engine. Generated engineering outputs retain the applicable human and licensed-professional review boundary.
