# AIAS Early Chat Analysis — Captures 0006–1699

The complementary analysis covers 1,694 chronologically ordered screenshots, from capture 6 through 1699. It closes the evidence gap immediately before the previously analyzed 1700–2588 segment.

## Recovered evolution

AIAS began this segment with a working BIM Property Inspector and then established a repository-first installation discipline: declared payloads, backups, targeted and complete tests, receipts and rollback. The architecture expanded through a live dependency/event graph, parametric BIM, structural objects, FEM, materials, steel and concrete design, geotechnics, civil infrastructure, IFC and vendor-facing adapters.

The conversation then corrected its own development strategy. It explicitly rejected disconnected libraries, terminal-only infrastructure, module-count inflation and documentation written after code. The approved destination became a unified professional product where a user can model, analyze, design, optimize and document one project, while all disciplines consume shared versioned engineering knowledge.

The final part of this range establishes the institutional foundation later materialized in AIAS: a development platform that builds capabilities, a Constitution and Engineering Manual, specification-driven development, executable quality gates, complete vertical slices, traceability, installers and reproducible releases.

## Repository reconciliation

The current repository materially supports the governance, SDD, asset registry, knowledge graph, development platform, capability factory, BIM/CAE foundations, installers, documentation, continuity and many domain engines described in the captures. `STRUCTURAL-CODES-PROGRAM-001` now preserves the recovered product destination and `SCP-02` provides the canonical structural-building contract.

The captures also contain historical claims of very large cumulative test counts and completed bundles. They remain historical evidence only. Current implementation status is determined from reproducible repository code, tests, manifests and releases—not from screenshots or narrative completion statements.

## Binding product objective

AIAS is a unified BIM/CAE engineering platform. Its permanent structural consumer is one complete building producing global analysis, beam design, column design, system optimization and a consolidated technical report. The same architecture extends to concrete, steel, timber, foundations, geotechnics, hydraulics, sanitation, stormwater, GIS, roads, earthworks, documentation, digital twins and governed external connectors.

Regulated output requires legitimate, versioned and applicable normative packs, verified benchmarks, professional review and jurisdictional approval. Generic engines or connector demonstrations never establish those claims by themselves.
