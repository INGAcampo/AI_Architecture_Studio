# PMO-000001 — AIAS Portfolio Management Office

The PMO turns the master inventory into dependency-aware portfolio candidates. It scores strategic value, urgency, reuse, acceleration and compliance while applying explicit risk and effort penalties. Only dependency-ready proposed items can be selected.

Seven portfolio stages are sequential and evidence-gated. Validation requires tests, quality gates, documentation, installer and release; benefit realization requires a baseline and measurement owner. Time reduction remains evidence-classified and is tested against the constitutional 45% target. The first real consumer is the current master inventory of incomplete AIAS concepts.
