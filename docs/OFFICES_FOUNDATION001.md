# OFFICES-FOUNDATION-001

This foundation institutionalizes the ten dependency-ready AIAS enterprise offices through immutable charters, unique accountability routing, decision rights, mandatory evidence, KPIs and escalation paths. Security-recovery and capability-automation work items are immediate real consumers.

AI Office is deliberately deferred: its approved dependency, `SYS-000012 Engineering Intelligence Core`, is not operational. This prevents a false organizational maturity claim. PMO remains operational under its existing dedicated implementation.
