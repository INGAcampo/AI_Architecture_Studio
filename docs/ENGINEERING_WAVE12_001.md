# ENGINEERING-WAVE12-001

This macro-delivery closes the five dependency-ready capabilities in roadmap wave 12: professional foundation workflow, jurisdiction-pack governance, hash-chained engineering evidence, differential validation and an anonymized licensed reference library.

The foundation workflow executes the real ECP calculation and code-check engines and joins their releases in an evidence ledger. The included jurisdiction pack is deliberately non-normative and reference-only; regulated use requires verified official packs and licensed professional review.

CAD/BIM Integration remains deferred because its approved dependency, Enterprise Drawing Framework, is not yet operational.
