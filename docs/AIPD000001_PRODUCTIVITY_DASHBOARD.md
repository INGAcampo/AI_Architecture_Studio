# AIPD-000001 — AIAS Productivity Dashboard

Permanent executive panel for technological capital, tests, SDD/constitutional compliance, documentation, sustainable acceleration, maturity, roadmap and the operating systems of the AIAS digital engineering company.

The dashboard distinguishes reference-case Level 5 capability from independently audited organizational Level 5 maturity. It never converts an engineering estimate into an audited claim.
