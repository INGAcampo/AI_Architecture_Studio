# Official Certification Framework Decision

Decision date: 2026-08-03.

AIAS adopts **CMMI Development V3.0 Maturity Level 5** as its official organizational maturity objective. **CMMI AIM** is the mandatory AI-specific benchmark extension. Neither result may be self-issued by AIAS.

AIAS also establishes an integrated management system targeting **ISO 9001**, **ISO/IEC 27001**, **ISO/IEC 42001**, and **ISO 22301**. ISO certificates must be issued by an independent competent certification body, preferably under recognized accreditation. ISO itself does not issue certificates.

The internal threshold of 365 observation days and ten real projects is an AIAS prudential readiness gate. It is not represented as an official CMMI requirement.
