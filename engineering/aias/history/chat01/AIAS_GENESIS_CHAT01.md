# AIAS Genesis — Chat 01

## Purpose and evidentiary status

This document reconstructs the founding intent of AI Architecture Studio from 155 screenshots supplied by the Project Director. It is a historical and architectural authority for intent, not proof that every package mentioned in the conversation was installed or that every proposed standard is legally applicable. Repository evidence, current official sources and professional validation remain mandatory.

## Origin

AIAS began as an effort to automate an architectural executive-project workflow from VS Code, Python and AutoCAD. The user explicitly stated that programming was not their specialty; the working model therefore became: the user acts as Project Director and domain decision-maker, while the assistant carries the technical implementation, documentation and installation burden with concrete, minimal user instructions.

The initial need quickly expanded from file automation into a proprietary professional platform. The decisive architectural idea was that AutoCAD, Revit, ETABS, SAP2000, Robot, Civil 3D, SketchUp and other tools must be connectors rather than the AIAS core. AIAS requires its own geometry model, intelligent engineering objects and software-neutral BIM/CAE representation.

## Founding identity

- Official name: **AI Architecture Studio**.
- Abbreviation: **AIAS**.
- Historical descriptor: **Professional CAD · BIM · Structural · AI Platform**.
- Enterprise direction: a digital-engineering platform, not merely another CAD application.
- Mission: assist professionals from initial idea through modelling, analysis, executive documentation and construction coordination in one environment.
- Long-term positioning: software, knowledge system, professional academy and multidisciplinary engineering enterprise.

The historical brand palette, typography, logo and splash-screen proposals are design candidates, not immutable constitutional rules. A formal brand system must be approved independently.

## Product thesis

AIAS is intended to let architects, engineers, surveyors, builders, students and specialist consultants work over one attributable project model. A professional should be able to find tools, technical knowledge, training, standards configuration, libraries and workflows in one ecosystem.

The platform must eventually support:

- CAD 2D/3D and professional documentation;
- architectural BIM and intelligent objects;
- structural concrete, steel, timber and masonry;
- geotechnics, foundations and soil–structure interaction;
- surveying, terrain, GIS, corridors and earthworks;
- hydrology, hydraulics, sanitary and stormwater networks;
- electrical, HVAC, fire protection and other MEP disciplines;
- urbanism, infrastructure and transportation;
- healthcare design and critical hospital systems;
- quantities, cost, schedules and construction control;
- visualization, digital twins and lifecycle information;
- AI-supported alternatives, checking, optimization and explanation.

## Intelligent-object doctrine

An AIAS object is not a disconnected drawing primitive. A wall, column, beam, slab or foundation must have stable identity and may carry geometry, level, material, analytical properties, normative context, quantities, cost, IFC identity, history and lifecycle data. The same object must serve 2D, 3D, BIM, calculations, documentation and exchange without uncontrolled duplication.

This doctrine is the bridge between the proprietary geometry engine, BIM, CAE, automation and the future digital twin.

## Standards and jurisdiction architecture

The founding chat explicitly required a standards manager independent from WALL, SLAB, COLUMN, BEAM and other commands. Jurisdiction, units, editions and discipline rules must be configurable without rewriting the application core.

The intended domains include architecture, structural design, loads, seismic design, geotechnics, sanitary and hydraulic systems, electrical systems, HVAC, fire protection, accessibility, hospitals, urbanism, construction quality and BIM interoperability.

The chat also preserved an important boundary: listing a code is not implementing it. Exact editions must be obtained and verified from official sources; formulas and checks require licensed, traceable implementation and professional validation. Venezuela is now the formally selected initial jurisdiction under profile VE-001.

## Development operating model

Every macrodelivery must include, in proportion to risk:

- specification before implementation;
- architecture and dependency analysis;
- functional code integrated with the real repository;
- automated tests and quality gates;
- documentation and change history;
- exact modified-file inventory;
- installation or deployment path;
- immediately usable evidence;
- compatibility and migration assessment;
- explicit professional and normative boundaries.

The chat used the term RFC for important decisions. AIAS has since evolved this into governed specifications, ADRs and ASDD/SDD artifacts. The intent remains: a future contributor must understand why every major decision was made.

## Documentation doctrine

Documentation must grow with the code. The founding proposal called for an Engineering Book covering system architecture, CAD, geometry, BIM, structural engineering, AI, API and plugins. Current living documentation, ADRs, ASDD, inventories and continuity records are implementations of that intent. Documentation created only to satisfy a checklist is prohibited.

## Internationalization

Internationalization is architectural, not cosmetic. Visible strings must use translation keys; Unicode, locale, date, decimal, paper, currency, time-zone and unit conventions must be separated from domain calculations. Internal base units must be stable. Language, jurisdiction and standards profiles must be independent but coordinated.

The historical language tiers included Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German and Italian first, followed by Russian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Arabic, and later Hindi, Dutch, Polish and Swedish. These are roadmap candidates; they are not all claimed as implemented.

## Knowledge Center and AIAS University

The Knowledge Center is intended to combine:

- general and module-specific manuals;
- downloadable manuals by country and language;
- contextual help tied to the active tool;
- official-source and edition-aware normative guidance;
- examples, templates, details, BIM families and components;
- offline PDF, DOCX, HTML and EPUB outputs;
- independently updateable country Knowledge Packs;
- an AI tutor grounded in installed modules, selected jurisdiction and authorized knowledge.

AIAS University extends the same system into structured professional education, with beginner through expert routes, exercises, assessments and credentials. Certification claims must remain governed and must not imply professional licensure.

## Geospatial and surveying foundation

The founding scope for survey professionals includes attributable XYZ points, GNSS and total-station data, point codes, coordinate reference systems, TIN surfaces, breaklines, contours, profiles, cross-sections, parcels, terrain comparison, cut/fill, drainage interpretation, setting out and reports. Exchange candidates include CSV/TXT, LandXML, DXF, GeoJSON and point clouds.

The terrain model is a shared multidisciplinary source for architecture, civil engineering, geotechnics, hydraulics, urbanism and construction. Coordinate-system provenance and transformation evidence are mandatory.

## AI doctrine

The AI component is not intended as a generic chatbot. It is an engineering orchestration and assistance layer operating over governed project objects, specifications, authorized knowledge and verification tools. It may generate alternatives and automate workflows, but must expose assumptions, sources, uncertainty and required human approvals.

## Value-delivery doctrine

The chat rejected waiting many years for usable results. Each sprint must answer:

1. What can the user do today that was impossible before?
2. How much professional time can the capability save?
3. Is it usable in a real, bounded workflow?

This historical doctrine is now consistent with AEC-000049, Materialización Inmediata del Valor.

## Historical roadmap synthesized

The screenshots contain several overlapping roadmaps rather than one final sequence. Their stable dependency logic is:

1. Application kernel, repository hygiene and professional workspace.
2. Geometry and proprietary CAD engine.
3. Architectural BIM and shared property system.
4. Structural, geotechnical and discipline engines.
5. Neutral BIM/IFC and governed vendor connectors.
6. Survey, terrain, civil, hydraulic and GIS platform.
7. Knowledge Center, AIAS University and country packs.
8. Engineering AI, optimization and automation.
9. Enterprise collaboration, cloud, marketplace and digital twin.

This dependency logic governs over historical version numbers or time estimates, which were inconsistent across screenshots.

- A download link or historical claim that a package was ready is not installation evidence.
- Current repository tests, manifests and integrity records determine materialization status.
- New findings from later chats may refine this history but may not silently erase it.
- Conflicts with the current Constitution, law, SDD or verified safety boundaries must be escalated and resolved explicitly.
