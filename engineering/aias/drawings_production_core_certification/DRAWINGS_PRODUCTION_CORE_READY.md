# DRAWINGS_PRODUCTION_CORE_READY

The canonical AIAS production chain now materializes design-bound drawing
models from Project Graph/BIM, passing analysis, versioned standards evidence,
and preliminary reinforcement design. Every reinforcement bar mark is assigned
to a structural sheet and carries its analysis, standards, design, and bar-set
SHA-256 lineage into the PDF model, QA/QC record, and issuance manifest.

This gate is certified only with `SYNTHETIC_TEST_DATA=true` and
`NOT_FOR_CONSTRUCTION=true`. The certification adapter never emits or claims a
native DWG. Real projects remain fail-closed without an authenticated baseline,
and construction detailing still requires licensed backend and professional
approval.

The automated flow reduces the modeled seven manual design-to-issuance
touchpoints to three governed checkpoints, an estimated 57.14% reduction.
